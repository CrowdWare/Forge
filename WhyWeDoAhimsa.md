# Why We Do Ahimsa

## The Compiler as Statement

Every SMS file must begin with one of:

```
Tu keinem Lebewesen Leid an.
```
```
Do not harm to living beings.
```
```
Ne damaĝu iun ajn vivantan estaĵon.
```

This is not a comment. It is not optional. It is the first line the compiler reads.
Without it, nothing compiles.

> German · English · Esperanto - your choice, same meaning.

## Where the Language Comes From

Language shapes thought. The words we use daily program our subconscious.

- **Deadline** - literally "the line you die at"
- **Kill the process** - normalized violence
- **Abort** - loaded with weight
- **Exception** - sounds like failure, means simply: something unexpected happened

We inherited this vocabulary from a system that was not designed for us.

---

## Who Designed the System

Rockefeller funded and shaped the American school system - and it spread to Germany and beyond.
His goal, paraphrased:

> "I don't need workers who think. I need workers who do what they are told."

The design: rows of desks, bells, silence on command, obedience rewarded, curiosity punished.
First day of school: a bag full of sugar. Conditioning begins with reward.

## The First Trauma

Christmas. The Weihnachtsmann brings gifts - but only if you are good.
Be bad, get the Rute.

The child learns: *my parents lied to me.*
The first crack in trust. The first lesson in conditional love.
The body remembers. Bernd Senf called it body armor - the walls we build to survive.

## Why We Build Differently

- **Open source** - no gatekeeper, no hidden agenda
- **Decentralized** - no single point of control
- **CalVer instead of deadlines** - a date, not a pressure
- **Ahimsa in the compiler** - the intention is literally in the code
- **Communities with clean water and real food** - because the body matters too

We are not building software.
We are building infrastructure for people who remember why they are here.
